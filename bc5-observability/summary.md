# why long-running agents need checkpoints

When long-running agents crash or get interrupted, they lose their in-memory state and must restart from the beginning, often reprocessing large amounts of data and losing track of completed work. Checkpoints solve this problem by creating consistent snapshots of an agent's state at specific times, allowing it to recover to a known good point and properly handle external system interactions. Without explicit checkpointing, agents cannot reliably resume from their last known state because they may have made irreversible changes to external systems or databases. Therefore, checkpointing is essential for maintaining consistency and enabling safe recovery in long-running agent systems.
